# 🎉 VIHANASA – Cultural & Techno Fest Website  
### Official Digital Platform of SRIT

VIHANASA is the **official cultural and techno fest website** of our college, built to serve as a **centralized digital platform** for managing, showcasing, and promoting all fest-related activities.

This project was **entirely designed, developed, and deployed**, and successfully hosted under the **official college domain**, making it a real-world, production-level system used by students and organizers during the fest.

## 📌 Project Overview

The VIHANASA platform was developed to eliminate scattered communication and provide a **single source of truth** for all fest-related information.

It enables:
- Easy discovery of events  
- Seamless participant registration  
- Centralized announcements  
- Engaging digital experience for users
